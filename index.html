Who we are

The University of Windsor's Formula SAE Electric team is made up of about 50 dedicated undergraduate students. We are committed to designing, building, and competing with a fully electric Formula-style race car at the annual SAE Formula Electric competition in Michigan.

Learn More

Our mission

Our mission is to create an inclusive workspace to inspire the next generation of automotive engineers. Although we always strive for the best, building up the members of our team for success is the long term goal of the team. By constantly pushing the limits of engineering and innovating creative solutions, not only does our vehicle evolve year after year, but our team members do as well.
