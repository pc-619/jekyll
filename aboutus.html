Our history

Our team is comprised of 3 team leads, 12 sub-team leads, and over 50 general members, all working toward the same goal of building a competitive, innovative, and reliable Formula SAE race car. Since its formation, the team has steadily grown in both size and capability, evolving into a well-structured and highly collaborative engineering organization.

Over the years, the team has refined its design and development processes, adopting industry-inspired workflows that emphasize iteration, validation, and performance-driven decision making. Each new generation of members builds upon the knowledge and experience of previous teams, continuously improving vehicle design, manufacturing techniques, and overall team efficiency.

The team's structure has also matured alongside its technical progress. What began as a small group of passionate students has developed into a multidisciplinary organization with clearly defined roles, responsibilities, and leadership systems. This evolution has enabled the team to take on increasingly complex engineering challenges while maintaining a strong focus on reliability and innovation.
